# Suggested 10-minute demo flow
1. **Orientation (1 min)** – header pills: model, embedder, reranker. Facts: 319 sections, 190 defined terms, 9 schedules.
2. **Revenue question** – "What royalty rates apply to onshore, shallow water and deep offshore crude oil production?"
   Show the rates table, click the `[Seventh Schedule, para. 10(2)]` chip → full text opens with (2) highlighted.
3. **Cross-reference** – "Explain section 306 and the provisions it relies on" → s.306 pulls in the Seventh Schedule automatically.
4. **Plain-language** – "How much must oil companies contribute to host communities?" → s.240, with definitions of *settlor* / *host communities*.
5. **Trust** – point to the verification bar (citations resolve, quotations verbatim). Open "Provisions consulted" and switch on *Inspector*.
6. **Honesty test** – ask something the Act does not answer, e.g. "What is today's petrol pump price?" The assistant says the Act does not address it.
7. **Follow-up** – "And for deep offshore?" after a royalty answer (conversation context is resolved).
8. **Browse** – open Chapter 4 Part II (Hydrocarbon Tax) from the tree.
